Somewhere, a tiger paces a
small enclosure, while elsewhere,
in a forest no one has ever
named, another tiger walks freely,
settling accounts with boars
and deer. One tiger pays for
the other, and neither knows it.
The lights above them
bloom like dandelions
coming apart
in the wind.
The cold had moved
to my teeth.
They held news.
I held a kitchen towel.
Morning reduced
some of the distances
in the kitchen.
