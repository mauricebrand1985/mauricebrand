Biography

Maurice Brand was born in Landgraaf, in the south of the Netherlands, in 1985. He read voraciously as a child, abandoned books almost entirely as a teenager, and fell back in love with literature in his thirties, this time for good. The novels he returned to were the ones that refused easy answers, and they are the ones he now tries to write.

—

Before fiction, there was business. For more than a decade, Maurice built and ran companies: a web design studio, an online marketing agency, and an e-commerce platform selling protein shakes. He holds a Master of Science in Management from the University of Huddersfield. The entrepreneurial years taught him discipline and how to finish what he started—useful habits for anyone who would later attempt a novel.

—

He writes literary fiction with a streak of psychological surrealism, circling grief, the impossibility of ever making the perfect choice, and the quiet absurdity of being a person at all. His work is shaped as much by Carl Jung as by any novelist, the slow labor of individuation, the shadow we refuse to look at, the personas we wear in its place. Among the writers he returns to are Haruki Murakami, Franz Kafka, Fyodor Dostoevsky, Albert Camus, George Orwell, and Cormac McCarthy: authors who treat the strange and the unbearable as simply part of the weather.

—

His debut novel, The Tiger in the Nameless Forest, is complete, and a handful of short stories can be read here on the site.

—

Maurice has lived in Bangkok since 2016, where he lives with his daughter. When he isn't writing, he lifts weights, swims, and walks the city, and is reliably comfortable in front of a big plate of grilled meat.
