Maurice Brand — writer of literary fantasy and mythic fiction. Loose papers on a desk, each bearing a handwritten fragment: between two silences; what the water keeps; the hour before the hour; north of the known; ink dries, the page stays; the body keeps its own time; and at the centre — stories are the things we carry across the dark so we do not lose ourselves.

Literary fiction, psychological surrealism,
and stories of grief, identity, and the difficult work of becoming.
